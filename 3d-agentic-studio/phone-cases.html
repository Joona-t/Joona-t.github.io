IH1 · our own case

Made from numbers, not from someone else's model

The shell is generated by a short script from the numbers on Apple's public dimensional drawing: the phone's size (iPhone 14 Pro Max: 77.58 × 160.71 × 7.85 mm), its corner curve and its edge profile. No third-party case geometry is used.

  • 0.25 mm clearance all round. Our first case was shorter inside than the phone; this one is 161.21 mm.
  • 2.0 mm back. Apple's drawing allows 2.1 mm at most, so MagSafe and wireless charging keep working.
  • Open buttons, open bottom, camera opening from Apple's numbers, and a lip that holds the phone without touching the glass.
  • Checked in 3D: the phone model overlaps the case by -0.0 mm³.

Two parts, because the slicer told us so: a sculpted back carved into a one-piece case starts printing in mid-air (“floating regions”). Printed as its own plate, relief up, it needs no supports. The plate drops into the frame from the inside and rests on a 3 mm ledge.

The lesson

Seven tries before our own case

  1. V1 engraved

    Three engraved cases sliced clean, then one was printed: too tight, the phone does not seat fully and an upper corner sits proud of the rim.

    Source: archive/iphone-14-pro-max-tpu-concepts/revisions/v2-fit-trial/README.md:5

  2. V2 fit trial

    Scaled about 0.5 % wider and 0.6 % longer around the centre as a controlled starting trial; Apple's body size (77.6 × 160.7 × 7.85 mm) showed V1 relied on stretch.

    Source: archive/iphone-14-pro-max-tpu-concepts/revisions/v2-fit-trial/README.md:19-21

  3. V3 length and relief

    Bottom-only length correction, deeper 1.2 mm channels and small detail samples to print first. No V3 physical print reported.

    Source: archive/iphone-14-pro-max-tpu-concepts/revisions/v3-length-and-relief/README.md:29

  4. V4 open buttons

    2 mm extension, corrected corners and exposed buttons, checked in a rigid 3D fit simulation. Physical fit pending.

    Source: archive/iphone-14-pro-max-tpu-concepts/README.md:3

  5. V5 open bottom

    Opened the bottom edge while keeping V4's fit and corner catches.

    Source: git log of the print archive: “Open TPU case bottoms while retaining V4 fit and corner catches”

  6. V6 rounded bumpers

    Softly rounded lower corner pads; all meshes watertight and all projects slice without warnings or supports.

    Source: archive/iphone-14-pro-max-tpu-concepts/revisions/v6-rounded-bumpers/FIT_V6.md:3-7

  7. Anime engraved V6

    The ten anime concepts redrawn as 1.0 mm-deep line engravings on the V6 case, plus 40 × 40 mm detail tests; all 20 projects slice without warnings.

    Source: archive/iphone-14-pro-max-tpu-concepts/revisions/anime-engraved-v6/README.md:5-11

  8. IH1, our own case

    A new shell designed from scratch from Apple's published dimensions (0.25 mm clearance, 2.0 mm back), with sculpted relief plates made from the concepts. Frame and plates slice clean with no supports. Not printed yet.

    Source: cases/iphone-14-pro-max/README.md

V1 to V6 were built on a third-party case shell (Sakkez Games, Standard Digital File License). We kept what they taught us, not their geometry: IH1 is designed from scratch, so its files are ours to share.

About this site

A one-off guide to creating things with your own AI agent, with 3D printing as the example. Made by Joona (Tyrninoksa), designing together with AI agents.

Fan art: many pieces here show characters I don't own (Code Geass, Bleach and others). They are made for fun, not official, and not for sale. AI images: concept pictures marked “AI-generated concept” were made with an image generator; prints and photos are real.
