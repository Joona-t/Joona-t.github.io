# Artwork, images and 3D models

The concept sheets, engraving stencils, plaque models (STL, 3MF) and images on this site are **fan art** of
characters from *Code Geass*. The characters and their designs belong to their rights holders. This is not an
official product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Joona-t (Tyrninoksa) shares these reconstructions for **personal, non-commercial use only**: look, learn, print one
for your own shelf. Do not sell the files or prints made from them.

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the code only. To build your own version, use your own images.
