BVBY ASH

バビー・アッシュ

Bvby Ash is a fun side project: concepts I make while I'm waiting on my other Claude Code sessions. When those are busy, I come here and make music, live-coded in Strudel and played from a neon booth in Cyber Space with a crew of seven Claudios on the decks.

It's world building, really. We already have characters and worlds for other projects and games, like the Kotoba language games for Japanese and Mandarin, so one day some of these tracks may end up in those games. For now it's just for fun and vibes.
